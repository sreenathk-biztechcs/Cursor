WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  When a rep marks a meeting as "No Show" in Odoo, Claude drafts a brief,
  gracious follow-up email. The tone changes based on how many times
  the client has no-showed.

  1ST NO-SHOW: Completely understanding. "Things come up." Easy reschedule.
  2ND NO-SHOW: Warm but note this is the second attempt. Ask if priority changed.
  3RD+ NO-SHOW: Very gentle. Offer to pause outreach if timing isn't right.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES APPLIED:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Max 120 words.
- Never say "you missed our meeting" — say "we didn't get to connect."
- Include a calendar link for easy reschedule.
- No guilt, no passive aggression.
- Sound like a real person.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE OUTPUT — 1st no-show:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subject: Didn't get to connect today

Hi [Name],

Looks like today's timing didn't work out — completely understandable.
Would love to find a slot that works better for you.

Here's my calendar in case it's easier to pick directly:
[CALENDAR LINK]

Happy to also reschedule via email if you have a preferred time.

Best,
[Rep Name]
[Designation]
