WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  When the presale team clicks "Request Proposal from AI" on a ticket
  (only available at "Ready for Proposal" stage), Claude generates a
  full sales proposal document.

  The proposal is written to a new Google Docs file, linked to the ticket.
  The rep reviews, edits, and shares with the client as a PDF.

  NOTE: This uses Claude Opus 4.6 — our highest quality model. It is
  ~5x more expensive than regular agents. Use only when truly ready.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROPOSAL PHILOSOPHY (what Claude is told):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every section must feel written FOR THIS CLIENT — not a template with placeholders.
Reference their specific requirements, industry, and stated challenges.
Use the client's own language and terms where possible.
Answer: "Why you, why now, and why this scope?"
Tone: confident, professional, consultative — not salesy.

RULES:
- No buzzwords: "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "revolutionary".
- No vague promises. Every claim must be specific.
- Pricing section: use the exact pricing table provided — do not add or modify.
- Executive Summary: must be strong enough to stand alone (read by a CEO).
- Language accessible to non-technical stakeholders.
- Total proposal: 1800–2500 words.
