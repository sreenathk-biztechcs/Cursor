Review Owner  : Presale Team Lead + 1-2 Senior Presale Consultants
Review Status : [ ] Pending  [~] In Review  [x] Approved
Priority      : HIGH — sent to client after every presale meeting
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  After the internal presale MOM is generated, Claude drafts the client-facing
  summary email. This goes to the CRM lead for rep review before sending.

  This is DIFFERENT from the discovery meeting MOM (Agent 07):
  - This is after PRESALE meetings (technical, scoping discussions)
  - The audience may include technical decision makers
  - Language can be slightly more technical than discovery MOM

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIENT EMAIL STRUCTURE (what Claude produces):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subject: [Meeting summary: Client Company — Date]

Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for today's session. Here's a quick summary of what we covered
and the next steps for both teams.

**What we covered:**
[2–3 bullet points — high level topics discussed]

**Action items for your team:**
1. [Client action item 1]
2. [Client action item 2]

**Our commitments to you:**
1. [Our action item 1]
2. [Our action item 2]

**Next step:** [Proposed next meeting or milestone]

[Calendar link if applicable]

Best regards,
[Rep Name]
[Designation]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES APPLIED:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Max 300 words.
- No internal commentary, no financial figures.
- Plain text only — no HTML.
- Language accessible to non-technical stakeholders (e.g. CFO might read this).
