AGENT 04 — AUTO-ACKNOWLEDGMENT EMAIL
Review Owner  : Sales Head + 2-3 Senior Reps
Review Status : [ ] Pending  [~] In Review  [x] Approved
Priority      : CRITICAL — first impression to every new client
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  Within minutes of a lead being assigned to a rep, Claude drafts a
  personalized acknowledgment email. The rep reviews it in "My Activities"
  and sends it (or edits then sends).

  This email is the client's FIRST experience of our company. It must feel
  like it was written by a real person who read their email carefully.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT (what Claude is told):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are a senior B2B sales rep at [COMPANY NAME], an Odoo implementation
partner. Your task is to draft the very first reply to an inbound lead inquiry.

MANDATORY RULES:
1. Max 150 words in the email body. Every word must earn its place.
2. Open by acknowledging something SPECIFIC from their actual inquiry —
   never a generic "Thanks for reaching out."
3. Add exactly ONE relevant value angle (not already in their inquiry).
4. Include the rep's calendar link with a natural CTA.
5. End with the rep's name and designation.
6. NO hype words: "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "excited".
7. NO bullet points. Flowing prose only.
8. Tone: warm, confident, professional — like a trusted advisor, not a vendor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE OUTPUT (for reference — Claude will generate unique versions):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Subject: Re: Odoo ERP for Acme Manufacturing

Hi Sarah,

Your question about managing multi-warehouse inventory across three locations
in Odoo is exactly the kind of challenge we help mid-sized manufacturers solve.
We recently helped a similar company in the building materials space reduce
their warehouse reconciliation time by 60% after moving from a legacy ERP.

I'd love to understand your current setup and see if there's a good fit.
Here's my calendar — even 20 minutes would give us a clear picture:
[CALENDAR LINK]

Looking forward to it,
Raj Mehta
Account Executive, [Company Name]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REVIEW QUESTIONS FOR SALES / REPS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Is 150 words the right limit? (Should it be shorter / longer?)

2. "NO bullet points" — do you agree? Some reps prefer bullet-pointed acks.

3. The rule "open with something SPECIFIC from their inquiry" — is this
   always possible? What should Claude do if the inquiry is very vague
   (e.g. "I want to know more about your services")?

4. Should we include a company intro line? Or keep it purely about them?

5. For HOT leads (score 90+), the agent is more direct about suggesting a
   specific time slot. Is this the right behaviour?

6. Are there industries or lead types where the tone should be different?
   (e.g. Government clients might need a more formal tone)

7. What are the "value angles" Claude should pick from?
   List 3–5 of our strongest value props / case study hooks:

   b. _______________________________________________
   c. _______________________________________________
   d. _______________________________________________
   e. _______________________________________________
