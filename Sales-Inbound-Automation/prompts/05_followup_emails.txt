Review Owner  : Sales Head + Senior Reps

Priority      : CRITICAL — 3 emails per lead, fully AI-generated
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  If a client doesn't reply after the auto-ack, Claude automatically drafts
  three follow-up emails on a schedule. The rep reviews and sends each one.

  DEFAULT SCHEDULE (adjust in the ICP scoring form):
    Hot leads  (90+): Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 after auto-ack
    Warm/Neutral    : Day 2 → Day 5 → Day 10 after auto-ack
    Cold leads (<50): Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14 after auto-ack

  KEY RULE: Each follow-up must use a DIFFERENT angle — never repeat what
  was said in the auto-ack or prior follow-ups.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOLLOW-UP 1 — Gentle Check-In (Day 2 for warm leads):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT RULES FOR FOLLOW-UP 1:
- Assume they were busy, not uninterested.
- Add ONE new angle not mentioned in the ack (e.g., a specific ROI signal,
  a relevant industry statistic, or a one-line case study reference).
- Keep it short: 80–100 words max.
- End with a soft CTA: "Would it make sense to jump on a 20-min call?"
- Do NOT resell. Be human.

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE:
  [Reference something time-relevant or industry-specific]
  [One new value angle — different from the ack]
  [Soft CTA + calendar link]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOLLOW-UP 2 — Value-Driven Nudge (Day 5 for warm leads):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT RULES FOR FOLLOW-UP 2:
- Be more specific about their industry or company.
- Reference one concrete outcome a similar client achieved (keep vague if needed).
- Ask ONE specific question to prompt a reply — not a yes/no question.
- 100–130 words max.
- CTA: suggest a time or share the calendar link.

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE:
  [Industry-specific insight or stat]
  [Case study hook — "A [industry] company similar to yours..."]
  [One open question that makes them think]
  [Calendar CTA]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOLLOW-UP 3 — Final Respectful Nudge (Day 10 for warm leads):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT RULES FOR FOLLOW-UP 3:
- Acknowledge this is the last nudge — but keep the door open.
- Softer tone: "I'll leave it here for now, but happy to reconnect when timing works."
- Offer a reduced ask: "Even 15 minutes would be enough to see if there's a fit."
- 90–120 words max.
- No pressure, no guilt. Professional and warm.

EXAMPLE STRUCTURE:
  [Brief reframe — come from a different angle entirely]
  [Leave the door open graciously]
  [Minimal CTA — low commitment]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES APPLIED TO ALL 3 FOLLOW-UPS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Never start with "I wanted to follow up" or "Just checking in."
2. Each follow-up must use a DIFFERENT angle from all previous emails.
3. Reference something specific from their original inquiry or company.
4. No bullet points. No hype. No generic phrases.
5. Sound like you genuinely believe you can help them — because you do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
