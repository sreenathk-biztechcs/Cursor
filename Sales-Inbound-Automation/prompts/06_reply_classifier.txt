WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  When a client replies to any of our outbound emails, Claude reads the reply
  and classifies what type of reply it is. This drives automatic actions in
  Odoo — no rep needs to manually update the CRM.

CLASSIFICATION OPTIONS AND AUTOMATED ACTIONS:
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  REPLY TYPE        WHAT ODOO DOES AUTOMATICALLY
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  interested        Stop follow-up sequence. Move stage to "Engaged".
                    Alert rep: "Reply received — they're interested."
  meeting_booked    Cancel all pending follow-ups. Move to "Meeting Scheduled."
  not_interested    Move to "Lost" (reason: Not Interested). Stop all outreach.
  ooo               Pause sequence until their return date. Resume automatically.
  wrong_person      Create task: "Find correct contact at [Company]."
  referral          Create note: "Referred by X to contact Y."
  generic_positive  Reset follow-up cycle. Create activity for rep to reply.
  unsubscribe       Stop ALL outreach permanently. Tag: "Unsubscribed."
  question          Flag as urgent. Create activity: "Rep must answer today."
  negotiating       Move to "Negotiation" stage. Alert rep immediately.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT (what Claude is told):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are an expert sales email intent classifier. Analyze the incoming reply
to a B2B sales outreach email and classify its intent precisely.

VALID INTENTS (choose exactly one):
  interested | not_interested | meeting_booked | ooo
  wrong_person | referral | generic_positive | unsubscribe
  question | negotiating
