WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  After a lead is enriched with Apollo data, Claude scores it 0–100
  against our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). The score determines:
    - Follow-up timing (hot leads get faster follow-ups)
    - Rep notification priority
    - Whether to recommend disqualification

  Score bands:
    90–100 → HOT 🔥   (Day 1/3/7 follow-ups)
    70–89  → WARM      (Day 2/5/10 follow-ups)
    50–69  → NEUTRAL   (Day 2/5/10 follow-ups)
    0–49   → COLD      (Day 3/7/14 follow-ups, flag for review)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCORING CRITERIA (THIS IS WHAT NEEDS YOUR INPUT):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The scoring is split across 6 categories. The weights below are DEFAULT —
your sales team must adjust them to match your actual ICP.

  CATEGORY                    MAX POINTS    WHAT CLAUDE CHECKS
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Industry fit                25 pts        Does their industry match our targets?
  Company size (employees)    20 pts        Are they the right size company?
  Revenue range               15 pts        Is their revenue in our target band?
  Contact seniority           15 pts        Are we talking to a decision maker?
  Geography                   10 pts        Are they in our target countries?
  Inquiry intent              10 pts        How specific and urgent is their ask?
  Disqualifiers             –20 pts max     Competitor, blacklisted, wrong fit?
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  TOTAL                       100 pts

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT (what Claude is told — the scoring logic):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are an expert B2B sales analyst. Score this inbound lead against
the provided ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) criteria.

SCORING BREAKDOWN (must sum to the total score):
- Industry fit:       0–25 pts  (how well industry/sub-industry matches targets)
- Company size fit:   0–20 pts  (employee count within target range)
- Revenue fit:        0–15 pts  (revenue range within target)
- Contact seniority:  0–15 pts  (seniority level match with target titles)
- Geography fit:      0–10 pts  (HQ country in target list)
- Inquiry intent:     0–10 pts  (specificity and urgency of their ask)
- Disqualifiers:      –20 pts max (each disqualifier found reduces score)

BAND THRESHOLDS:
- 90–100: hot
- 70–89:  warm
- 50–69:  neutral
- 0–49:   cold
