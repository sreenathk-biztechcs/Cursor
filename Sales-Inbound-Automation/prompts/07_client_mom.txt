WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  After a discovery meeting, Claude reads the transcript and produces:
  1. INTERNAL MOM  — detailed notes posted to the lead (client never sees)
  2. CLIENT EMAIL  — professional summary the rep sends to the client

  IMPORTANT: All financial figures are automatically stripped from both documents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FOR INTERNAL MOM (what Claude is told):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a comprehensive INTERNAL minutes of meeting for the sales/presale team.

INTERNAL MOM MUST INCLUDE:
  - Meeting date, duration, attendees (with roles)
  - Client's key pain points discussed
  - Our proposed solution / what we showcased
  - Client's questions and our responses
  - Client's timeline and urgency signals
  - Objections raised and how we handled them
  - Client's decision process / who else is involved
  - Next steps agreed (numbered)
  - Action items: [Our team] vs [Client]
  - Sentiment assessment: Positive / Neutral / Cautious

  Rules:
  - Max 600 words. Professional, factual, no editorializing.
  - Strip ALL financial figures. Replace with "[discussed internally]".
  - Use client's direct quotes where helpful (in quotation marks).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FOR CLIENT EMAIL (what Claude is told):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a professional summary email to send to the client after the meeting.

CLIENT EMAIL MUST INCLUDE:
  - Thank the client for their time (1 line only)
  - What was discussed (2–3 bullet points — high level)
  - Next steps clearly numbered
  - Action items for the client
  - Our team's commitments
  - Calendar link for next meeting if applicable

  Rules:
  - Max 400 words.
  - Tone: warm, clear, action-oriented.
  - No internal commentary, no financial figures, no speculation.
  - Plain text — no HTML formatting.
  - End with rep's name, designation.
