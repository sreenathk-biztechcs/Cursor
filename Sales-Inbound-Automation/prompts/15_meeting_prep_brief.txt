Review Owner  : Sales Head + Senior Reps

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  2 hours before any scheduled CRM meeting, Claude generates a "prep brief"
  for the rep. Posted as an internal note on the lead — rep sees it in
  "My Activities" before the call.

  Goal: Rep walks into the call knowing exactly what to cover, what to ask,
  and what objections to prepare for.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PREP BRIEF STRUCTURE (what Claude produces):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Meeting Prep — [CLIENT] — [Meeting Type] — [DATE]
Duration: X min | Contact: [name, role]

### Client Snapshot (30 seconds)
[Industry, size, what they do, why they reached out — 3 lines max]

### What They're Trying to Achieve
[Their stated + inferred goal for this meeting]

### Top 3 Talking Points
1. [Most important thing to land]
2. [Second point]
3. [Third point]

### Likely Objections & How to Handle
- [Objection]: [Brief handling hint]

### Questions You Must Ask Today
- [Specific discovery/qualifying questions]

### What Success Looks Like After This Call
[What's the ideal outcome — next step agreed, requirement clarified, etc.]

### Quick Context Reminder
[1–2 lines on how we got here — relevant email/prior meeting context]

REVIEW QUESTIONS FOR SALES / REPS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Is this brief useful enough to be worth the automation?
   Or do reps prefer to prep themselves?
   [ ] Very useful — please build this
   [ ] Useful but not priority
   [ ] Reps prefer to prep themselves — skip this

2. 2 hours before the meeting — is this the right timing?
   [ ] 24 hours before (allows more prep time)
   [ ] 2 hours before (current)
   [ ] 30 minutes before

3. "Likely Objections & How to Handle" — is it useful for Claude to suggest
   how to handle objections? Or should it just list the objections?

4. "Questions You Must Ask" — should these be personalized per client,
   or should there be a standard question bank?
   [ ] Fully personalized (current)
   [ ] From a standard bank you approve
   [ ] Combination: standard + personalized additions

5. For a 2nd or 3rd meeting with the same client, should the brief
   include a "Prior Commitments" section?
   (e.g., "In the last meeting, you said you'd send them a case study — did you?")
   [ ] Yes — accountability reminder
   [ ] No — keep it forward-looking

6. Should the brief include a recommended close move for this meeting?
   (e.g., "This meeting: close for a presale ticket" or "This meeting: ask for budget approval")
   [ ] Yes — helpful nudge
   [ ] No — too prescriptive
