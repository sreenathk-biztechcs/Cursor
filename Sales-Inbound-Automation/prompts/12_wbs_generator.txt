WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  When the presale team clicks "Generate WBS" on a presale ticket, Claude
  reads all the requirements from presale MOMs and generates a full WBS.
  The WBS is written to your Google Sheets template as a new tab.
  The tech team then fills in effort estimates.

  Claude follows your existing WBS template phase structure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WBS OUTPUT STRUCTURE (each task looks like this):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase: Phase 1 — Discovery & Gap Analysis
  Task P1.T1:
    Name       : Business requirements workshop
    Description: Facilitate structured requirements sessions with client
    Owner      : BA / Presale
    Depends on : — (first task)
    Effort     : TBD by tech team
    Deliverable: Signed requirements document
    Notes      : Critical path item — all subsequent phases depend on this

Phase: Phase 2 — System Design & Prototype
  Task P2.T1:
    Name       : Odoo architecture design
    ...

[And so on for all phases]
