AGENT 09 — DAILY PRESALE AGENDA (10:30 AM Standup)
Review Owner  : Presale Team Lead
Review Status : [ ] Pending  [~] In Review  [x] Approved
Priority      : MEDIUM — runs daily on all active presale tickets
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  Every morning at 10:00 AM, Claude reads each active presale ticket's
  recent activity and posts a "today's agenda" note on the ticket.
  All ticket subscribers receive a notification.

  This replaces the need for the PM/presale lead to manually prep the
  10:30 AM standup agenda for each deal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AGENDA STRUCTURE (what Claude produces):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Daily Agenda — [DATE]

**Status:** [One line: where are we in the presale process?]

**Today's Goal:** [What must be achieved today — be specific]

**Carry-over Items (from last meeting):**
- [Open action items not yet resolved]

**Questions to Resolve Today:**
- [Any open questions that need answers]

**Prep Needed Before Next Meeting:** *(only if meeting is today or tomorrow)*
- [Documents, demos, technical prep required]

**Watch:** [Any risk or blocker that needs attention]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES APPLIED:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Max 200 words total.
- Be specific — no generic placeholders like "follow up with client."
- If all action items are done and next meeting is >3 days away, keep it brief.
- If no activity for 7+ days and no meeting scheduled: flag as URGENT.
