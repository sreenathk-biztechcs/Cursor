WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  After a lead is classified as Genuine, Playwright scrapes the client's
  profile from Apollo.io. Claude then takes the raw Apollo data dump and
  structures it into 30 CRM fields in Odoo.

  This means reps see a pre-filled lead record — no manual data entry.

FIELDS POPULATED (30 total):
  Company: Industry, Sub-industry, Employee count, Revenue range, HQ country,
           HQ city, Founded year, Tech stack, Funding stage, LinkedIn URL, Website
  Contact: Job title, Seniority, Email (verified), Phone, LinkedIn URL,
           Decision-making authority
  Lead:    Likely pain point (inferred), ICP match signals, ICP disqualify signals,
           Inquiry category, Inquiry urgency, Enrichment confidence score,
           Apollo conversation history, Follow-up angle, and more.
