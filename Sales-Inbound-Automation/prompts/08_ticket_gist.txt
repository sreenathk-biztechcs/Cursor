AGENT 08 — TICKET GIST (Presale Onboarding Summary)
Review Owner  : Presale Team Lead
Review Status : [ ] Pending  [~] In Review  [x] Approved
Priority      : HIGH — first thing presale team reads on every ticket
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  When a presale ticket is created, Claude reads the ENTIRE lead history
  (all emails, internal notes, meeting MOMs, enrichment data) and writes
  a structured summary — the "Gist" — for the presale team.

  Goal: A new presale team member can read the Gist and be fully briefed
  in 2 minutes without reading through all the emails.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GIST STRUCTURE (what Claude produces):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Client Overview
[Company, industry, size, location — 2 sentences]

## What They're Looking For
[What they want — be specific, use their own words where possible]

## Key Requirements Identified
[Bulleted list — be concrete, not vague]

## Prior Discussions Summary
[Timeline of key touchpoints — what was said, what was agreed]

## Open Questions (must resolve in first presale meeting)
[Numbered list]

## Budget & Timeline Signals
[What was hinted at — NO specific $ amounts. Describe as "tight/moderate/flexible"]

## Risk Flags
[Any concerns: vague requirements, decision committee, competitor mentioned, etc.]

## Recommended Presale Approach
[How to structure the first presale meeting]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REVIEW QUESTIONS FOR PRESALE TEAM:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Is this structure right for your presale process?
   Are there sections you want added or removed?
   Add sections: _______________________________________________
   Remove sections: _______________________________________________

2. "Budget & Timeline Signals" — no specific $ amounts. Correct?
   Or should the internal gist include financial figures?

3. "Recommended Presale Approach" — should Claude suggest this?
   Or is that too presumptuous and should be left to the presale team?

4. How long should the Gist be?
   [ ] 200–300 words (very brief)
   [ ] 400–600 words (current target)
   [ ] 600–900 words (comprehensive)

5. Should the Gist include a confidence rating on the requirements?
   (e.g. "Requirement 1: HIGH confidence — clearly stated in email")

6. What is the most important thing the presale team needs to know
   when they first pick up a ticket?
   _______________________________________________

7. Are there any internal company terms or classifications we use for
   presale tickets that Claude should be aware of?
   _______________________________________________
