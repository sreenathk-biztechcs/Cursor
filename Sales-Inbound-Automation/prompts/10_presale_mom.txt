AGENT 10 — PRESALE INTERNAL MOM (Post-Presale Meeting)
Review Owner  : Presale Team Lead
Review Status : [ ] Pending  [~] In Review  [x] Approved
Priority      : HIGH — documents every presale meeting on the ticket
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS AGENT DOES:
  After each presale meeting, Claude reads the transcript and generates
  a detailed INTERNAL MOM. This goes to the presale ticket (not the lead).
  Client never sees this.

  IMPORTANT: Financial figures are stripped. Technical details are kept.

  The ticket # is identified from the transcript — reps say
  "Moving to ticket 1042" during the meeting as a verbal cue.
